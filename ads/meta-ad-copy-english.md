## Targeting suggestions (both editions)

- **Interests:** Christianity, Bible study, Christian ministry, devotionals, faith-based pages/publishers (e.g., YouVersion, She Reads Truth / He Reads Truth — check current Meta interest availability), church attendance behaviors.
- **Age/gender:** split by gender per edition; broad 25–55 is a reasonable starting range, narrow after data comes in.
- **Placements:** start with Advantage+ placements; Instagram Reels and Facebook feed tend to perform well for this kind of product.
- **Budget:** start small ($10–20/day) per ad set with 3–4 creative variants, let it run 3–4 days before judging performance (don't kill ads too early).

## Meta compliance notes

- Don't phrase copy as if you know something personal about the viewer (e.g., avoid "Are YOU struggling with X" framing) — Meta's Personal Attributes policy prohibits implying you know a viewer's health, financial, or personal situation. The copy above stays general/aspirational, which is compliant.
- Religious-interest targeting for a religious product is standard and not a "Special Ad Category" (those are housing, employment, credit, and social issues/elections) — no special targeting restrictions apply here beyond normal ad policy.
- Avoid "before/after" or exaggerated life-change claims (e.g., "this will fix your marriage/anxiety") — keep claims to what the product actually is: a reading plan and journal.
- Make sure the landing page clearly states it's a digital, non-refundable-after-download product (or your actual return policy) to avoid disputes.

## Before you can launch

I can't create the Meta ad campaign itself — that requires your own Meta Business Manager login and a payment method on your account. Steps you'll need to do:
1. Create/confirm a Facebook Page for the brand.
2. Set up Meta Business Manager + an ad account (business.facebook.com).
3. Set up checkout for the product (Gumroad, Payhip, or Stripe + a simple delivery email) since the landing pages here don't have a live payment button yet — I only built the front-end.
4. Upload the landing page (e.g., host `landing-page/english-men.html` / `english-women.html` via GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your checkout platform's hosted page).
5. Create the campaign in Ads Manager, paste in the copy/headlines above, upload creative, set targeting and budget from the suggestions above.
